# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Keyboard-first terminal users who run several TUI applications and want to move between them without managing separate terminal sessions or losing process state.

## Product Purpose

tuimux centralizes terminal applications in one persistent, navigable workspace. It succeeds when starting, finding, switching, arranging, and stopping apps feels immediate while the embedded application remains the visual and operational focus.

## Brand Personality

Calm, dense, and precise. The interface should feel terminal-native and capable, with quiet chrome and direct language that stays out of the user's workflow.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboard-like decoration, opaque chrome that hides terminal theming, bright traffic-light status rows, oversized controls, and novelty interactions that slow keyboard operation.

## Design Principles

- Keep the active application, not the multiplexer, at the center of attention.
- Make state and focus legible without adding persistent visual noise.
- Preserve fast, predictable keyboard workflows across layouts and sidebar positions.
- Use density to improve scanning, not to crowd or decorate the interface.
- Let terminal themes and transparency remain part of the user's environment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Communicate important state with color plus shape or text, never color alone. Preserve readable contrast across built-in and custom themes, support terminal-safe glyphs, and avoid motion-dependent feedback.
